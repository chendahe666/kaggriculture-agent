# K320 experiments

These files are intentionally small ablations of the downloaded K320 artifact:

- `k320-no-preempt.py`: disables preemptive premium-product sales.
- `k320-no-family-counters.py`: disables two route-family counters.
- `k320-conservative.py`: applies both changes.

All three variants performed worse than original K320 in the four-game screening panel against COK V10. They remain here as negative results and were not copied into `candidate/main.py`.
